# Highlights

- Injection–recovery tests calibrate what a tree-ring array can detect before use
- 13 Japanese ITRDB sites, 3,193 site–event pairs: no far-field earthquake signal found
- M8 subduction earthquakes at 100–250 km suppressed stand growth by <7–9 % (95 %)
- Independent-year nulls overstate significance when events are shared across sites
- ~140 sites of 30 trees would be needed to see a 10 % far-field signal at 3σ
